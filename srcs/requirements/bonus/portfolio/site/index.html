About

I'm a software engineering student at 1337, the Moroccan campus of the 42 Network. There are no lectures and no teachers in the usual sense - the curriculum is built around peer-to-peer learning, project-based work, and figuring things out largely on your own, with code review standing in for grading.

Most of what I know, I learned by building something, breaking it, and reading until I understood why. That applies as much to debugging a container network at 2am as it does to picking apart an unfamiliar framework for the first time. I've gotten comfortable with not knowing something yet.

I'm drawn to the layers underneath the interface - Linux, infrastructure, backend systems - but I like full-stack work just as much, and lately I've been spending time on computer vision and applied AI. What connects it all is the same instinct: open it up and see how it actually works.
